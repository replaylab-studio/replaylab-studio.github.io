Privacy Policy - Replay Lab Factory

Last updated: November 20, 2026.

Replay Lab Factory is an internal tool used by Replay Lab to publish its own videos on its own accounts: the YouTube channel Wasted Every Day (youtube.com/@wasted.everyday) and the TikTok account Wasted Every Day (tiktok.com/@wasted.everyday). It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed. Through the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API, the tool uploads videos to our own accounts and reads the basic profile and public statistics of our own videos (views, likes and comment counts). On TikTok it uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish only for our own account.

Use of data. Only to publish our own content and measure its performance. No data about other users is collected, sold or shared.

Storage. Authorization tokens are stored locally on our own computer and are never shared with anyone.

Revoking access. Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and TikTok access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services).

YouTube API Services. This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
